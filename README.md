🎧 Taskbar Dancer

A cheerful little desktop companion that lounges on top of your Windows taskbar and starts grooving the moment system audio plays.

It hooks directly into the Windows Master Audio Peak Meter (`pycaw`). It doesn't rely on microphones or ambient noise thresholds—whether you're listening on headphones or speakers, playing games, or watching YouTube/Spotify, it detects digital audio output instantly.
